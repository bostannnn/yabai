What the redesign missed

I ran 0.17.1 (main 962ecc29, debug build) on the tablet emulator at 800 dp portrait and 1280 dp landscape in Graphite, over the 21-book Local library, and swept the code for every dialog, sheet, popup and message the app can show. Sections 8.0–8.9 covered every screen on the boards. Seven surfaces never reached a board, and the biggest is the Finished screen you see at the end of every book. Seven more are gaps on screens that were redesigned, and three are things the app doesn't have yet. Each card below shows today's screen next to a proposal. Pick Do, Maybe or Don't. Picks save to this page, the same way the earlier boards worked.

Group A

Surfaces that were never on a board

These are moments everyone reaches: finishing, opening, the first second after launch, and the small confirmations in between. None of them was mocked in the second pass, so they still use the first pass's look, or Material's.

reader-core · journalM

Today

  • It's a card inside a dialog. "Finished" appears twice, once as the title and once on the card. "Local · EPUB" is file information, not something a reader needs at this moment.

A2 · Opening a book, and when it can't open

reader-core · all readersS–M

Today

A4 · The first second: splash and window background

appS

Today

  • No splash is configured: no core-splashscreen and no windowSplashScreen*. The theme is Theme.AppCompat.DayNight with no windowBackground. Android draws its own white splash, which then fades into a dark app.

Proposal

  • Add core-splashscreen. The background is the saved theme's background colour and the icon is the launcher mark. On Android 13+, SplashScreen.setSplashScreenTheme remembers a light or dark splash style each time the theme changes, so the next cold start already matches. Older versions follow values-night.
  • Set windowBackground to the same colour so no white frame shows before Compose draws.
  • Exit: the icon fades as the library header's title comes in on effects. No custom animation beyond that.

feature:library-shelvesS–M

Today

  • Its empty state is plain text ("No shelf items"), not AppEmptyState.

  • Empty shelves use AppEmptyState: "Nothing in progress" with "Browse the library".

Group B

Polish on screens the second pass already redesigned

These are gaps that showed up on the emulator today on screens the boards already covered, plus the reader's own page, which the boards covered only through its menus.

B1 · The book page doesn't know what you thought of the book

feature:library · journalM

Today

  • Below the top block, a local book has one fact, "Format EPUB", and then empty space.
  • Your rating and note live only in the journal. The book page never shows them, even for a book you finished and rated.
  • "Remove file" is a full-width button with the same weight as Continue.

Proposal

  • Add a "Your reading" section built from the journal: the stars (the same editor as A1, tap to rate), your note, "Started 18 Oct · Finished 24 Oct", sessions and time, and "Read again" once finished.
  • Local books also get the facts the file has: pages or chapters, the date added, and the series and number from ComicInfo.
  • Remove file becomes a quiet row at the end of the page ("Remove from library", in the error colour) and keeps its confirm dialog.

B6 · Places that bypass the kit

several modulesS

Today (code)

Group C

Things that would make it feel native to current Android

None of these exist today. They are where an app reads as current rather than just tidy.

C1 · Downloads you can see outside the app

app · downloadsM

Today (code)

  • The app has no notification channel and no notification of any kind. RemoteDownloadWorker is a plain CoroutineWorker with no foreground notice. Leave the app during a 24-file download and you can't see it, and Android may stop the work.

Proposal

  • A "Downloads" channel. A progress notification shows the series cover, "Star Courier · 12 of 24 files" and Stop. When it finishes, "Ready to read" opens the book page.
  • The first download asks for notification permission (Android 13+) in an AppDialog that says why, never at launch.

C2 · Continue reading from the home screen

app (new Glance module)S, then L

Today

  • Long-pressing the icon shows only Android's defaults. There is no widget.

Proposal

  • Step 1 (S): dynamic shortcuts. "Continue Glass Meridian" with its cover as the icon, plus Search and Journal.
  • Step 2 (L): a "Continue reading" widget in Glance. The cover, the title and the progress, tinted by the cover colour, with a tap to open. At larger sizes it shows the In progress strip.

C3 · A small haptic vocabulary

core:ui-componentsS

Today

  • Only the journal's stars give haptic feedback (JournalRatingEditor.kt).

Proposal

  • AppHaptics in the kit:
    • a light tick when the dock's slider crosses a chapter mark
    • a confirm on Finished (A1)
    • a tick on long-press selection
    • a tick on segment changes and on reaching the end of a drag-to-reorder
  • All of them follow the system's touch-feedback setting.

Not design, but you'll notice

Features a reader app of 2026 has and yabai doesn't

These need product decisions before any design work, so they have no picks. In the EPUB reader there is no search inside the book, no bookmarks, and no highlights or notes. A long press goes to Android's own text selection (PageCurlDragGesture.kt:32), and there is no dictionary or translate panel of the app's own. Each of these would get its own board.
