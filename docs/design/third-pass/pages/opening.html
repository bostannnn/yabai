Opening a book, and when it can't open

You marked this Maybe on the review, so this board decides it. Opening a book today goes through a dark frame into a blank page under the bars, and on a slow first open it says "Opening EPUB with Readium…". When a book can't open, each reader says it differently, often in developer words, and the comics and PDF readers offer no way forward. Card 1 picks the opening look (A or B); the rest are Do, Maybe or Don't. Screens are drawn at real sizes (1 px = 1 dp, scaled to fit) on the 800 dp tablet with 9.6's page colours. What's there today (main with 9.6, debug build, emulator, the Local library)

  • The EPUB reader shows EpubReaderMessage three times before the page ("Restoring EPUB state…", "Opening EPUB with Readium…", "Restoring EPUB position…"), on the app's background with a spinner and a Back text button. Its errors use the same screen with the reason and Back.
  • The comics reader shows its page source's diagnostic as centred text with the title under it, and no Back. The same text covers "still loading" ("Downloaded comic pages are still being inspected") and real failures ("Downloaded comic file was not found", "No download data source for this comic").
  • The PDF reader keeps its bars and puts "Unable to open this PDF." or "No PDF document available." where the page would be, with no retry.
  • A format no reader takes gets the old top bar and "No reader is registered for MOBI." (ReaderHost.kt).
  • I couldn't make an error happen on the emulator (a local book reads the app's own copy), so the error cards draw today's screens from the code.

1 · One opening for every reader: the book, or just the page

pick A or B

A. The page colour, the cover, the title and author, and a thin line that moves.

B. Only the page colour, with the line moving along the top.

  • One opening state in reader-core for the EPUB, comic and PDF readers, on the colour the book will open on: the book's page colour, or the comics canvas.
  • A: the cover at 132 × 198 dp (from the library's cover cache), the title at 22 sp and the author under it, and a 120 dp line of 3 dp whose bright part slides across every 1.2 s. It tells you which book is coming, and on a slow open there is something to look at.
  • B: nothing in the middle; the line runs along the top edge, where the top bar will be. Quieter, and the page arrives in the same place it was waiting.
  • No bars and no system bars until the page is there. Back still leaves.

2 · No words for a quick open, plain ones for a slow one

reader-core

After 600 ms, one line under the author (drawn as A).

  • Nothing is written for the first 600 ms, so a quick open is only the book and the line.
  • After that, one line that says what is happening: "Getting it from Kavita…" while a server book's file comes down, "Opening…" while the file is read, "Finding your page…" while the reader looks for your place.
  • Gone: "Restoring EPUB state…", "Opening EPUB with Readium…", "Restoring EPUB position…" and the spinner.

3 · The opening fades into the page, with no dark frame

reader-core

Left to right: opening, half-way (about 100 ms), the page with its bars.

  • The opening state stays until the first page has drawn its text, then cross-fades into it on the effects curve (210 ms). The blank page under the bars is gone.
  • Everything is on the book's page colour from the first frame, so a Paper or Sepia book never passes through dark.
  • The bars come in with the page and stay until you tap, as today.

4 · When a book can't open: one screen with a way forward

AppEmptyState

Today, drawn from the code: the EPUB reader, the comics reader, the PDF reader.

Proposed: a server book that didn't answer (Black), and a local file that is gone (Sepia).

  • Every reader shows the same screen: the reader's top bar with Back and the title, then AppEmptyState in the middle on the book's page colour: the books drawing, "Couldn't open this book", the reason in a sentence, and the actions.
  • Try again (filled, in the page's text colour) runs the opening again; Back to library (tonal) leaves. A local file that is gone has no Try again: it gets Back to library only, and the reason says what to do.
  • Taps on the page area do nothing here (today a tap on the PDF error can turn pages that don't exist).

6 · A comic whose pages are still coming shows the opening, not an error

reader-comics

Today "…still being inspected" as centred text; proposed the opening state on the comics canvas.

  • "Downloaded comic pages are still being inspected" and "…are not ready" mean the pages are on their way: the opening state stays (with "Opening…" after 600 ms).
  • The other diagnostics are failures and get card 4's screen with card 5's words.

7 · A kind of file yabai can't open

ReaderHost

Today the old top bar and "No reader is registered for MOBI."; proposed the same error screen.

  • "yabai can't open MOBI files" over "It reads EPUB, PDF, CBZ and CBR.", with Back to library only.
  • On the app's background (there is no page colour for a book that can't open), with the reader's top bar.

8 · "Download again" for a server book whose download is broken

app

A downloaded Kavita comic whose file is gone.

  • When a downloaded server book's file is gone or unreadable, the error screen offers Download again (filled) beside Back to library: it removes the broken download and starts a fresh one, with the download toast from 9.2; the book opens when it is done.
  • Without it, the way out is the book page's Remove download and then Download.
