yabai · redesign plan 9.2 · board · 24 October 2026

Toasts

You picked Do for one confirmation style across the app. Today two messages exist, both Material snackbars: "Updated to x.y.z" and the progress-sync failure. Many actions give no answer at all, and the download queue writes messages that never reach the screen. Below is the toast itself, where it sits, and the messages it would carry. Card 1 picks the look (the rest draw A). Toasts are drawn at real sizes (1 px = 1 dp, scaled to fit) over emulator screenshots in Graphite. Picks save to this page.

What's there today

  • AppSnackbar wraps Material's Snackbar in its default inverseSurface colours. Its two callers place it themselves, bottom centre, and dismiss it on their own timers.
  • No answer: Mark finished and Mark unread (server books), Remove download, removing a book or a journal entry after their confirm dialogs, and every download request. RemoteDownloadQueueController builds "Queued 3 files for download.", "Download already queued for …" and "Selected items must come from one source." and they are thrown away in AppRemoteDownloadController.
  • Importing opens the book straight away; a file it can't read gets the "Unsupported file" dialog.

1 · The toast: a raised pill, or glass like the bars

pick A or B

A. A raised pill in surfaceContainerHighest with a soft shadow.

B. The bars' glass: blurred, at their 0.86 tint, with a hairline edge.

Both in Paper: A, then B.

  • A 48 dp pill (two lines: 64 dp, 24 dp corners) with a 20 dp icon, the message in 14 SemiBold and, when there is one, an action at the end: a 36 dp pill in the accent at 14% with the accent's text.
  • A is the kit's own surface, quiet on any backdrop. B matches the reader's bars and the drawer, and needs the blur (Haze) over whatever is under it, which costs a little on a scrolling grid.
  • Errors keep the same pill; only the icon turns error.

2 · Where it sits, how long it stays

AppToastHost

Tablet: centred over the content, never wider than 480 dp.

Phone: the width less 16 dp each side.

In a book: 12 dp above the dock while the bars show; with them hidden, above the page number.

  • 16 dp above the navigation bar, centred in the content beside the rail (not under it).
  • Anything floating at the bottom moves up out of its way while it shows: the grid's scroll-to-top button rises above it, on spatial.
  • 4 seconds, 6 with an action or for an error. A new toast replaces the one showing (it drops away as the new one rises), so they never stack. Swipe it away sideways or down. Touching it keeps it up.
  • It rises 16 dp and fades in on spatial / effects, and fades out sliding 8 dp down. Screen readers hear it as a polite announcement; with an action it waits for TalkBack users instead of timing out.
  • One host for the app (AppToastHost in the kit, fed by a small queue in app), replacing AppSnackbar.

3 · Downloads say what they did

RemoteDownloadQueueController

After Download on a selection, over the grid.

The other answers, at 0.8 scale.

  • "Downloading Star Courier #2" or "Downloading 3 issues", with View (opens the Downloads sheet).
  • "Star Courier #2 is already downloading" with View, "Star Courier #2 is already on this device".
  • "Pick books from one source to download them together" as an error (the selection mixes sources; today nothing happens).
  • The queue's own strings ("Queued 1 file for download.") are replaced by these; the result types stay.

4 · Mark finished and Mark unread: with Undo or without

pick A or B

A. With Undo. (Server books only; the Local page has no such button.)

A above, B below: the message alone.

  • "Marked as finished" / "Marked as unread", with the book's title when the toast is not over its page ("Marked Star Courier #2 as finished").
  • A needs data work: Undo puts back the progress from before the change and, for Mark unread, the journal events it deleted (today the use case deletes them for good, review item #15). The change is held until the toast leaves, or snapshotted and restored.
  • B is a plain confirmation; the change stays as now and the button on the page flips back if you want to reverse it.

6 · When progress can't reach a server

AppProgressSyncNoticeController

The library after leaving a book.

The wording, one source and several.

  • The same pill with the error icon instead of a red slab, 6 s, with Retry (the sync page's Retry now). Two lines when the reason is useful.
  • Wording for people: "Couldn't send your progress to Kavita" (today "Unable to sync Kavita progress: …"), "Couldn't send your journal to Grimmory", "Couldn't send your progress to Kavita and Grimmory". The reason goes on the second line only when it says something actionable ("Signed out: sign in again").
  • Offline sources stay quiet, as today: their uploads wait and the sync page shows them.
