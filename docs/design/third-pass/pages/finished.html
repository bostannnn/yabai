The Finished screen

You picked Do for this on the review board. Here it is in detail, drawn at real sizes (1 px = 1 dp, scaled to fit) in Graphite with the emulator's real covers and the colours the app's cover-colour code gives them. The first card picks the layout. The cards after it draw layout A, and B would carry the same pieces. As before, choose Do, Maybe or Don't on each card, add a note if you like, and your picks save to this page.

What changes

  • One surface, with no card inside the dialog and no repeated "Finished". It is tinted by the cover the way the reading card and the book page already are (Modifier.coverTint, AppCoverAccentTheme).
  • The book's own numbers from the journal: JournalItemSummary already has the start and finish dates, sessionCount and totalReadingTimeMillis.
  • The journal's star editor and the review keep their behaviour: a star saves on tap, and the note saves when the screen closes. They only change look.
  • "Local · EPUB" and "No rating" go. JournalItemReviewCard is deleted, because nothing else uses it.
  • No haptic, since you said Don't to haptics. Motion only.

A, card

  • 440 dp wide, 28 dp corners and 24 dp padding over the 45% scrim, centred. The bars stay under the scrim.
  • A 112 × 168 dp cover beside the overline (11.5 Bold, accent), the title (26 / 30 Bold) and the author.
  • × closes it and keeps you on the last page.

B, end page

  • The screen is the cover tint. A 200 × 300 dp cover and a 40 / 44 Bold title, both centred. The numbers are four 34 Bold figures, and the stars are 48 dp.
  • It reads as an ending, like the last page of a printed book, but it is a full screen to leave every time.

Nothing recorded on this device (finished elsewhere and synced): the row is left out.

  • 32 dp pills at 12.5 SemiBold with tabular figures, in the card's text colour at 10%. They wrap on phones.
  • "Days" counts calendar days from the first session or progress event to now: "6 days", or "Read in one sitting" for a single session. Time is "4 h 12 min", or "38 min" under an hour. Pages are left out, because the page count is already on the book page.
  • "9th in October" counts the books, or the comics for a comic, finished this month, matching the journal's Month view. It is hidden for the first one.
  • A pure, unit-tested function over JournalItemSummary and the month's overview, in feature:journal.

3 · Rating and a private note

JournalRatingEditor

Rated 3.5. A second tap on a star makes it a half, as today.

The note opened.

  • "Your rating" (13 SemiBold) with the hint at the end of the line: "Tap to rate", then "Tap again for a half", then the value ("3.5").
  • 40 dp stars in the cover accent. Empty ones are the card's text colour at 28%.
  • The note starts as a 48 dp pill, "Add a private note", with a pencil. Tapped, it opens into a 12 dp-corner field of three lines labelled "Private note", with the placeholder "Only you can see this" (the journal sheet's words). It saves when the screen closes, as today.
  • A note written earlier opens the field straight away, showing its first two lines.

4 · What comes next

AppReaderCoordinator

A comic with a next issue, in Star Courier's colours.

Nothing next: Back to library is the filled button.

  • A 20 dp-corner strip (black at 22% on dark tints, white at 55% on light) holds the next cover (56 × 84 dp), "Up next · 2 of 3" or "Up next in list name · 3 of 7", the title, and a filled "Read next" pill in the cover accent.
  • Under it, "Back to library" is tonal. When nothing is next, it becomes the filled button.
  • The next item is the one today's "Start next" uses: the next unread in a Kavita list, or the next in a server series. Local comics get none, because Local series were a Don't.

AppSheet

412 dp phone: a bottom sheet over the last page.

  • Under 600 dp the card becomes a bottom sheet: full width, 28 dp top corners, the kit's handle, 20 dp padding. Drag it down or tap the page to close it.

6 · In a light theme

appCoverColors(dark = false)

Paper theme, Light page, The Salt Orchard.

  • Light themes get the cover's pale tints (about L 0.90) under dark text, and the accent darkened to about L 0.35 with white text, the same as the reading card in Paper.
  • The scrim drops to 32%, as everywhere in light themes. The pills and the note sit on the text colour at 8%.

7 · How it arrives

AppMotion
At device speed
  • The scrim fades in. The card scales from 0.96 and fades on effects. The cover rises 12 dp on spatial. The pills fade in 160 ms later. The stars pop in from 0.6 on spatialFast, 50 ms apart.
  • It is quiet: no confetti, no sound and no haptic. With animations off in Android, it simply appears.
  • Closing reverses the card and the scrim only.

8 · When it opens (the review's Maybe)

pick A or B
A. As today: on reaching the end
  1. EPUB and comics: the moment the last position is reached, by reading, by dragging the dock, or by picking the last chapter in Contents.
  2. PDF: on "next" from the last page.
B. On turning past the end
  1. Reaching the last page marks the book finished, as today, and the dock reads 100%.
  2. The screen opens when you turn once more: a swipe or tap past the last page, as PDF already works.
  3. A jump with the dock or Contents opens nothing, so you can still read an afterword or the notes.
  4. The dock's 100% stays tappable and opens it at any time.
