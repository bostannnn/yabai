# Translation in the reader

Select text in an EPUB and the card opens by itself; the toolbar over the text keeps **Copy** (Readium hands the whole toolbar to the app; `EpubSelectionActionModeCallback`). With Settings › Translation › Open when I select text off, the toolbar says **Translate** and **Copy** instead, and Translate opens the card or the sheet. Translation runs on the device (Google ML Kit, feature:translation); a language is a one-time download of about 30 MB.

- **Selected text** is filled with one see-through blue, `rgb(91 134 196 / .45)`, on every page colour, and keeps its own text colour: 5.4:1 or better on Paper, Sepia, Grey, Dusk and Black (`EpubSelectionHighlightTest`). Readium's own opaque pale blue left Dusk and Black unreadable (1.0:1).
- **One to three words open the card** (`AppAnchoredCard`) at the words: From and To as tonal pills with a down arrow and the swap between them, ×; the selection in `on-surface-variant`, the translation at 24 SemiBold, then "In this sentence" and the sentence it sits in, translated. Footer: `tablet_android` 16 "Translated on this device", Copy, Open full.
- **The card follows the selection**: drag a handle and, once it rests (300 ms), the card translates the longer text in place, at any length; your language picks stay. A passage in the card reads at 16 Regular, its source cut to two lines, the body scrolling past 320. Tapping the page closes the card and clears the selection; closing the card clears it too.
- **With Translate (switch off), longer selections and Open full open the sheet**; with the switch on only Open full does: the reader's floating panel at the end edge (glass, like Contents and Settings), "Translation" with ×, the same language row, Selected, a hairline, the language's name and the translation. The footer sits at the bottom: Copy translation (filled) and Open in… (tonal), which hands the text to any app that translates selected text.
- **From** is the book's language (its details), English when they don't say; **To** is your language from Settings › Translation (the device's by default); a book already in your language goes into the other language you used last. Either pill opens the language menu: a search field, "On this tablet", then "Download · about 30 MB each". A pick is remembered.
- **States** in the same surface: a language not downloaded asks first (Not now / Download), downloading shows an indeterminate bar and "You can keep reading", translating shows skeleton lines, a failure says what happened with Close / Try again.
- **DeepL, with your own key** (online, optional). The card's footer starts with a **chip naming who translates**: `public` 14 "DeepL" or `tablet_android` 14 "This tablet", with a down arrow. Its menu, "Translate with": DeepL (or "Add DeepL…" before a key, which opens the key dialog) and This tablet ("Private, offline"); the pick is Settings' Translate with, so it holds for later cards. DeepL needs no download. When DeepL can't help (offline, over the month's limit, the key refused, or a language it doesn't have) the tablet translates instead, the chip says This tablet and a caption under the translation says why ("DeepL couldn't be reached, so this tablet translated").
- **Settings › Translation** (hub group Reading): **Languages**: Translate into (a value row that opens the language menu), Open when I select text (a switch, on by default; "Off: select text, then tap Translate"). **Translate with**: DeepL ("Better wording. Sends the selected text and its sentence to DeepL"; before a key it opens the key dialog) and This tablet ("Private and offline. Used whenever DeepL can't help"), the chosen one `selected` with a check. **DeepL** (with a key): API key ("Free plan · ••••3f2a:fx", Change) and This month (an `AppProgressBar`, "41,200 of 500,000 characters"). **On this tablet**: each language with Remove (English is built in), Add a language.
- **The key dialog** (`AppDialog`): "Translate with DeepL", what it is and the free plan's 500,000 characters a month, an `AppTextField` "API key" (supporting: where to find it, kept on this tablet only), the privacy line "What you select, and the sentence around it, goes to DeepL. Nothing else from your books does." Buttons: Remove key (only when changing a saved one), Cancel, Check and save (a spinner while DeepL checks; a refused key says so in the field).
