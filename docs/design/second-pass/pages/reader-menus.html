Today

Where today's reader menus get in each other's way

The rule

Bars stay put, panels fit between them

  1. The bars never get covered.A panel opens in the space between the top bar and the bottom bar. Back, the title and the progress bar stay visible and usable.
  2. The button shows what's open.The button that opened a panel stays visible and filled while the panel is open, and tapping it again closes the panel.
  3. Same places in every reader.Bottom left is where you are (Contents for books, Pages for comics). Top right is how it looks (Aa for books, the settings icon for comics). One panel at a time; tapping the page closes it.
  4. Each bar says one thing.The top bar names the book and the chapter or issue. The bottom bar holds the position: progress, the slider and the page. Comics keep their page strip inside the bottom bar, not as a second box.

On a phone the panels rise from the bottom as sheets. The top bar stays, and the bottom bar steps aside while a sheet is open, because a sheet and the slider can't both fit.
